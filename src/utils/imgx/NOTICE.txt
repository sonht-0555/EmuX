Emux IMGX browser decoder

worker.js is generated from station/moetruyen-worker/src/browser/worker-entry.js
and the shared IMGX V3/V4 decoder. It uses Web Crypto and @noble/ciphers 1.3.0.

IMGX layouts and AEGIS/AES-SIV/secretstream adaptations follow Keiyoushi's
MoeTruyen extension (Apache-2.0). See Apache-2.0.txt and the repository's
station/moetruyen-worker/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for attribution and references.

@noble/ciphers by Paul Miller is MIT licensed. See Noble-MIT.txt.

No source keys, cookies, grants, or images are bundled in this decoder.
